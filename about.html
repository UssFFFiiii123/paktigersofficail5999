About us

Discipline, strength and self-defence under certified black belt coaches.

Pak Tigers Martial Arts and Fitness Club teaches discipline, strength and self-defence in a safe, well-run environment. Our students train under internationally certified Kukkiwon black belt coaches.

Whether you are starting for the first time, preparing for tournaments or just want to get fit, there is a class for your age group and level.

  • Styles taught
    Taekwondo, Bredan Fou, self-defence
  • Who can join
    Men, women, teenagers and kids
  • Branches
    Dhoke Chaudrian and Bahria Town
  • Coaches
    Black belt certified, including a female coach

Pak Tigers Martial Arts and Fitness Club teaches Taekwondo, Bredan Fou and self-defence under certified black belt coaches in Rawalpindi.
